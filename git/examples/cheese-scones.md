# Cheese scones

Source: Edmonds Cookery Book, 51st De Luxe Edition, 2003.

## Ingredients

3 cups plain flour                         6 teaspoons baking powder
1/4 teaspoon salt                          75 g butter
1 -- 1.5 cups milk, approximately          extra milk
3/4 cup grated cheese                      extra grated cheese
pinch cayenne pepper                       pinch mustard powder

## Instructions

Sift flour, baking powder, salt, cayenne pepper and mustard powder into a bowl.
Cut butter in until it resembles fine breadcrumbs.  Mix in grated cheese.  Add
milk and mix quickly with a knife to a soft dough.  Knead a few times.  Lightly
dust an oven tray with flour.  Press scone dough onto this.  Cut into 12
even-sized pieces.  Leave a 2 cm space between scones.  Brush tops with milk.
Sprinkle each scone with a small amount of extra grated cheese.  Bake at 220
degrees C for 10 minutes or until golden brown.
