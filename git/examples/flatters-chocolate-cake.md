# Fool-proof chocolate cake

Source: The New Zealand Flatters Cookbook, 1995.

## Ingredients

1.5 cups white flour                       1 cup sugar
4 tablespoons cocoa                        2 teaspoons baking powder
1 teaspoon cinnamon                        0.5 teaspoon salt
0.33 cups oil                              1 tablespoon vinegar
1 teaspoon vanilla essence                 1 cup water

## Instructions

Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, salt and cinnamon together,
then add the oil, vinegar, vanilla, and water.  Stir this rather
unpromising-looking mixture up until nearly all the lumps are gone and
it has a nice thick pouring consistency.  (Do not *ever* beat a cake
batter once the flour and liquid have both been added, as this toughens
the texture of the end product.)

Pour the mixture into a lined cake tin, about 20 cm square, and bake at
180 degrees C for 25--30 minutes.  It is done when the centre is firm to
the touch, and the sides have pulled away a little from the tin.
