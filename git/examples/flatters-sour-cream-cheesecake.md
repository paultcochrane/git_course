# Sour cream cheesecake

## Ingredients

### Crust

1.5 cups plain or malt biscuit crumbs      0.5 teaspoons cinnamon
3 tablespoons sugar                        75 g butter, melted

### Filling

250 g cream cheese                         2 tablespoons flour
2 eggs                                     0.5 teaspoons vanilla essence
0.5 cups sugar                             25 g butter, melted
500 g sour cream

## Instructions

Combine the biscuit crumbs, sugar, cinnamon and melted butter, and press
into a square 20 cm or round 23 cm tin or dish.

Allow the cream cheese to reach room temperature, then beat it about a bit
until it's creamy.  Add the eggs, sugar, sour cream, flour, vanilla and the
25 g melted butter.  Pour onto the crumb base and bake at 160 degrees C for
45-55 minutes.  Check it after about 20 minutes, as you may want to put some
foil loosely over it if the top has already browned a bit.
